# Kingdom of Science — Quotes & Catchphrases Reference
*Dr. Stone · Compiled from anime, manga, and fandom sources*

---

## SENKU ISHIGAMI

**Primary catchphrase:** *"This is exhilarating!"*
His go-to expression of scientific excitement. The English dub renders it as *"Get excited!"* It closes out Season 1 and appears throughout the series constantly.

**Hyperbolic intensifier:** *"10 billion percent!"*
His recurring substitute for "absolutely" or "certainly." Used in both confident declarations and emotional moments (e.g. *"I was ten billion percent certain you were alive"*).

> *"I'm going to use the power of science to rescue every single person."*
Core mission statement.

> *"Humanity isn't going down that easily. Two million years of human history is right here inside me."*

> *"It's not that there are things science can't explain. You just look for the rules behind those things. Science is just a name for the steady, pain-in-the-ass effort that goes into that."*
One of his most quoted lines, defining his entire worldview.

> *"A man who praises a man to his face is full of ulterior motives."*
His cynical read on flattery, foreshadowing his wariness of Tsukasa.

> *"There is no such thing as a 'useless' person in the Kingdom of Science."*
Recurring theme, spoken when integrating members like Suika and Kaseki.

> *"All I can do is improvise one step at a time."*

> *"Just look, Tsukasa. No matter if you kill me or anyone else, science will revive itself. With these hairless apes, even a crazy idiot who endlessly tests things can surely revive it again!"*
One of his most defiant lines.

> *"I'm going to beat fantasy with science."*

---

## TAIJU OKI

**Recurring:** *"I will do the heavy lifting!"*
His self-defined role, said often and earnestly.

> *"I don't have the strength to fight, and I don't have the brains to lead. But I have the heart to keep going until the job is done."*
His clearest self-summary.

*Note: His ongoing confession to Yuzuriha is his defining motivation and is referenced constantly by other characters as a running joke and a source of warmth.*

---

## YUZURIHA OGAWA

**Recurring:** *"Craft is all about perseverance!"*
Her quiet philosophy, fitting her meticulous nature.

*Note: Her quotes are sparsely documented compared to other main cast. She is defined more by silent action than spoken lines, which is itself very on-brand for her character.*

---

## CHROME

**Catchphrase:** *"Damn!"* (Japanese: *"Yabe!"*)
Said constantly — practically his verbal tic. He says it so frequently it's become one of the series' most recognized recurring lines.

**Recurring insult/nickname (affectionate):** *"Gorilla!"*
His go-to word for Kohaku when she does something physically absurd.

> *"Science is the only way to level the playing field between a genius and a regular person."*
His realization of what Senku's "sorcery" actually is.

---

## KOHAKU

> *"My blade may find that sharp tongue of yours before either of us knows it."*
Her intro-era warning, characteristic of her blunt aggression.

**Recurring sentiments:** *"I'm gonna save Ruri-nee!"* / *"I believe in Senku!"*
Two phrases that sum up her dual loyalty throughout the series.

*Note: Her internal monologues about science as a collaborative human endeavor are some of the more philosophical lines in the series, but they read as passages rather than quotable one-liners.*

---

## KASEKI

**Catchphrase:** *"Now you've got me fired up!"*
Triggered every time Senku presents him with a new impossible fabrication challenge. Practically a Pavlovian response — often accompanied by him tearing his shirt off to reveal improbable musculature.

*Note: His joy at new engineering problems is his defining character beat. The quotes matter less than the recurring physical reaction they produce.*

---

## GEN ASAGIRI

**Running verbal habit:** *"Senku-chan~♫"*
His teasing, sing-song way of addressing Senku. The tilde and musical note are essentially canon punctuation for this line.

**Recurring line:** *"It's time for the mentalist's job!"*
Said before executing a deception plan.

> *"No matter which one of you falls, I'll be riding the winning horse."*
His early self-serving declaration of neutrality (Chapter 23).

> *"I'm a shallow man. I don't care about logic or the future. I just want what I want. And right now, I want a bottle of cola."*
The line that gets him to defect — one of the series' funniest character moments.

**Xeno's assessment of him:**
> *"Either he's honest to a fault, or the world's greatest liar."*
Arguably the best one-sentence character summary in the entire series.

---

## SUIKA

> *"Since I'm always wearing this thing on my head, I'm never able to be useful to anyone. I'll probably be the same when I grow up. That's why whenever Suika sees someone who needs help—"*
Her defining inner monologue from early in the series, establishing her desperate desire to contribute.

*Note: Her arc is defined by action over words. Her solo survival chapters are almost entirely without dialogue, which makes them hit significantly harder.*

---

## KINRO

**Catchphrase:** *"Rules are rules."*
Said flatly, without elaboration, repeatedly. It is his entire worldview compressed into three words. His guard-post password challenges are all riffs on this same philosophy.

---

## GINRO

**Recurring move:** *"Secret Technique: Leave It To Somebody Else!"*
Announced as though it is a legitimate martial arts technique. One of the series' best recurring jokes — his cowardly declarations are all riffs on this energy, making excuses sound heroic.

---

## RURI

*Note: Her most important "quote" is the entire 100 Tales — a message encoded across 3,700 years. Less a catchphrase, more a living legacy. Her warmth is expressed through how she speaks to others rather than through individually quotable lines. Documented quotes are thin.*

---

## MAGMA

*Note: His most consistent contribution is proposing to **kill** every new enemy as the group's first discussion point — treated as a running joke. "Just kill them" is functionally his catchphrase. No single widely-documented memorable standalone quote, but his blunt pragmatism is consistently played for both comedy and occasional effectiveness.*

---

## KOKUYO

*Note: Sparse documented quotes. He is defined by gravitas and silence. His endorsement of Senku carries weight precisely because he speaks rarely.*

---

## RYUSUI NANAMI

**Catchphrase:** *"I desire it!"*
Used for everything he wants — ships, women, adventure, freedom. "Desire" is his entire personality compressed into one word.

> *"I want it all — every last bit of this world!"*
His mission statement.

> *"I love all women, you see, so naturally that includes you. Men too!! I desire them all!!"*
From Chapter 142. Characteristically chaotic and earnest.

---

## FRANCOIS

**Catchphrase:** *"Desire = Justice!"*
Their complete philosophical endorsement of Ryusui's greed, delivered with total sincerity. Their dynamic with Ryusui in two words.

**Secondary reliable line:** *"Leave it to me."*
Said before handling whatever impossible logistical task is required.

---

## UKYO SAIONJI

> *"I won't let anyone die — not even the enemy."*
His pacifist creed. Confirmed as his defining line.

*Note: Documented unique quotes are relatively sparse beyond this. His character is expressed more through action — precision non-lethal archery, his superhuman hearing — than through monologue.*

---

## NIKKI HANADA

*Note: Her Lillian Weinberg devotion is her defining character trait. "Lillian Weinberg is a goddess" captures her energy accurately, though exact phrasing varies by translation. No single widely-cited standalone quote beyond the Lillian voice authentication scene.*

---

## MINAMI HOKUTOZAI

*Note: Sparse documented quotes. Her journalist instinct and documentation role define her more than any single line.*

---

## YO UEI

*Note: His self-serving declarations are consistent but not condensed into a single catchphrase. His cowardly/mercenary commentary is played for laughs similarly to Ginro.*

---

## TSUKASA SHISHIO

**Verbal tic (Japanese):** *"Ymm"*
He adds this sound to almost all of his sentences in the Japanese version. Subtle but consistent enough that fans catalogued it.

> *"This stone world is a paradise not yet to be tainted! Let's just revive the young people — we'll live together with nature, which doesn't belong to someone else. It's an opportunity to cleanse humanity!"*
His ideology in full (Chapter 4).

> *"You can call it killing, if you want. It's a matter of perception. I'm culling humanity, for the sake of a new world."*

> *"I will kill you, Senku. But I will not lie to you."*
His paradoxical code of honor at its clearest.

> *"I'll promise you one thing: you two will never be in danger again."*
Said to Taiju and Yuzuriha, exposing his warped paternalism.

---

## HYOGA

**Catchphrase:** *"Proper."*
Said to compliment something executed with precision and elegance. He uses it sincerely, which makes it more unsettling than if it were ironic. He compliments the Kingdom of Science on doing things "properly" even while attacking them.

*Note: His dialogue is otherwise sparse and cold. He communicates through action and tactical observation more than speech.*

---

## HOMURA MOMIJI

*Note: Almost entirely silent. Her devotion to Hyoga is expressed through presence and action, not words. No widely documented catchphrase or quote.*

---

## KIRISAME

*Note: Sparse documented quotes. Her defining character moment is her decision to defect upon learning of Ibara's betrayal — action over words.*

---

## SOYUZ

*Note: No widely documented catchphrase. Defined by his quiet determination to reclaim his home.*

---

## AMARYLLIS

*Note: Her confidence and vanity are expressed through manner rather than a single recurring line. No widely documented catchphrase.*

---

## MATSUKAZE

**Catchphrase:** *"My Lord!"*
Directed at Ginro, with complete sincerity, at every opportunity. The comedy is in how utterly earnest he is about it.

---

## MOZ

*Note: No widely documented catchphrase. His self-serving mercenary attitude parallels Gen's early declarations but delivered with considerably more physical menace.*

---

## XENO HOUSTON WINGFIELD

*Note: His most-cited line is actually his assessment of Gen:*
> *"Either he's honest to a fault, or the world's greatest liar."*

His ideology parallels Tsukasa's elitism but through scientific gatekeeping rather than physical violence. No single widely-cited catchphrase of his own.

---

## STANLEY SNYDER

> *"I follow orders. That's all."*
His defining self-summary. Minimal dialogue overall — he is defined by precision action rather than speech.

---

## BRODY DUDLEY

> *"There isn't a single person on this planet who deserves to die!"*
Confirmed, spoken in Episode 69. His most important line and the one that defines his entire character arc — the moral conscience of the American Colony.

---

## JOEL GEAR

*Note: His perfectionism is his personality, but no single widely-documented quote. His obsessive precision with mechanical work is his character expression rather than any particular line.*

---

## BYAKUYA ISHIGAMI

> *"If you're serious, you can accomplish anything through diligent application of science."*
One of the series' foundational quotes, passed down as a guiding principle.

> *"I'm sure you're not the kind of kid to watch these broadcasts... but I swear I'll bring you back home a mountain of scientific souvenirs from space!"*
His pre-petrification broadcast to Senku, one of the more emotional moments in his backstory.

*Note: His final message to Senku (encoded in the 100 Tales) is the emotional peak of his character arc. Less a single quote, more a letter across millennia. His dying words frame love as the cornerstone of civilization.*

---

## LILLIAN WEINBERG

*Note: Her songs are her quotes — they persist across 3,700 years. The music itself is the message. No single spoken line is widely documented from her; she speaks through culture, not dialogue.*
